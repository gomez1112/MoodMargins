## Platform emphasis

- **iPhone:** prioritize quick check-in, autosave, and useful Insights. Readable UI is more useful than filling the entire screen with decoration.
- **iPad:** show the genuine wide workspace and page browser/editor together. Keep the capture legible; do not imply a second feature that is absent on the device.
- **Mac:** retain actual Mac window controls, sidebar, and desktop layout. Use real Mac captures, never an iPad screen inside a desktop frame. Candidate first headline: “A mood journal at home on Mac.” / “Tu diario de ánimo, en el Mac.” / “يوميات مزاجك على Mac.”
- **Watch:** use native Watch screenshots; avoid poster text overlays. Keep the horizontal mood selector, quick note, Recent pages, and detail screens. Prioritize distinct workflows over three nearly identical emotion selections. A sync claim requires an actual two-device verification; do not fabricate a shared-data demonstration.
- **Vision Pro:** retain genuine visionOS windows and spatial context. Candidate description/headline direction: “A little space for your day.” / “Un espacio para tu día.” / “مساحة صغيرة ليومك.” A native Mac or iPad image is not a substitute. No immersive-world claim is proposed.

Before a future refresh, re-check actual current UI and each uploaded set; the repository's historical contact sheet is not proof that a later build looks identical. Preserve the user's manually edited assets until a replacement set is explicitly reviewed.
