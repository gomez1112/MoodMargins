Your daily mood journal, one small moment at a time.

Choose an animated mood sticker, write a few words, and let your pages save automatically. MoodMargins gives you a notebook for how you feel, without needing to write a long entry.

CHECK IN AND LOOK BACK
• Express your mood with five animated stickers.
• Keep notes on lined notebook pages that save as you write.
• Find past entries with search, mood filters, and tags.
• Explore mood trends over 7, 14, or 30 days, or a year.
• See your longest journaling streak and most common mood.

FROM WRIST TO NOTEBOOK
Check in on iPhone or iPad, or choose a mood and add a quick note on the Apple Watch companion. Your Watch moods and notes sync with the main app through your private iCloud account when iCloud is available and enabled. Use the same Apple Account on your devices; changes can take time to appear.

FREE TO START, YOURS TO PERSONALIZE
Daily journaling, standard Insights, and the Pastel theme are free. Choose light or dark appearance. Make the notebook your own with Botanical, Coastal, or Sunset: purchase a theme once to keep it, or access all premium themes while subscribed to MoodMargins Plus.

A LITTLE HELP WITH PLUS
Plus includes AI tag suggestions, recaps of your saved pages, premium themes, and an ad-free experience. Optional AI requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and ready. It tries Apple's Private Cloud Compute first, with an on-device fallback. Turn off cloud processing in Customize to use on-device AI. Cloud use requires internet and is subject to Apple's availability and usage limits.

YOUR JOURNAL AND YOUR CHOICES
No MoodMargins account is needed. Pages are stored on your device, with private iCloud sync when available and enabled. The developer does not operate a server that receives your journal. Free users can see app recommendations in Customize; Plus is ad-free.

Weekly, monthly, and yearly Plus subscriptions renew automatically and include the same features. Individual theme purchases are permanent and do not include AI. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Ending Plus keeps your entries and themes purchased separately.

MoodMargins is for personal reflection, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
