MoodMargins is a personal mood journal. No MoodMargins sign-in or demo credentials are required. An Apple Account is used only for optional iCloud sync and Apple purchases.

New in version 1.1: Customize → Feedback & ideas presents the Kickstart Signal board in a navigation sheet. Only voluntarily submitted feedback is sent to that service; private journal content is not submitted. A Kickstart Exchange advertising banner appears below About for free accounts after purchase entitlements load. Plus accounts remain ad-free. Neither integration is used for tracking, and Signal receives no premium-status flag.

Customize → Rate this app opens this app's App Store write-review page on devices. Automatic native StoreKit review requests become eligible after three distinct saved Today check-in days and wait until editing has finished. Requests are limited to once per version, with at least 14 days and three additional check-in days between attempts. All moods count equally. StoreKit may suppress a request, and TestFlight does not show automatic prompts.

Choose a mood or type a note in Today or Pages. Changes save automatically after a short typing pause and when leaving the editor. Browse or delete entries in Pages. Insights contains free mood trends, mood stickers, longest streak, and tag/activity patterns.

Customize contains native StoreKit SubscriptionStoreView for weekly, monthly, and yearly MoodMargins Plus plans, and ProductView for individual Botanical, Coastal, and Sunset themes. Every plan unlocks the same features: all premium themes, AI tag suggestions, and AI recaps. Individual themes are permanent non-consumable purchases and do not unlock AI. Restore purchases is in Customize; subscription management and privacy/terms links are also provided.

AI uses Apple's Foundation Models. Private Cloud Compute is on by default, with an off switch in Customize; a failed cloud request retries on device. Apple Intelligence must be enabled and ready. If AI is unavailable, free journaling and standard Insights continue working. The app is not a medical or diagnostic service.

Main-app data uses a private iCloud store when available. The Apple Watch companion now uses the same SwiftData journal models and private CloudKit container as the main app. Moods and notes autosave locally and sync automatically through the same iCloud account when available. Existing local Watch pages are migrated without overwriting separate phone pages. The Watch updates only fields the person edits, preserving phone tags and other journal details. Sync timing depends on iCloud connectivity and system scheduling. Bundled mood animations are rendered with the existing dotLottie library. Marketing images contain fictional entries; no preview controls or sample data are included in release builds.
