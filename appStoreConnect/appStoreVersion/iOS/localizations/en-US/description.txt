A mood journal for the little moments that make your day.

Choose an animated mood sticker, write a few lines, and build a daily diary that feels like yours. MoodMargins saves your pages automatically, so you can focus on how you feel.

CHECK IN YOUR WAY
• Express your mood with five animated stickers.
• Write on soft, lined notebook pages.
• Organize moments with tags and find entries by date, mood, or search.

UNDERSTAND YOUR PATTERNS
Explore mood trends across 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, or a year. See your longest journaling streak, most common mood, and frequently used tags. Daily journaling and standard Insights are free.

MAKE IT YOURS
Start with the free Pastel theme, or choose Botanical, Coastal, and Sunset. Each palette includes light and dark appearances. Buy a theme once to keep it, or enjoy all premium themes with MoodMargins Plus.

A LITTLE HELP WITH PLUS
Plus adds AI tag suggestions and recaps of your saved pages. AI requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and ready. It uses Apple's Private Cloud Compute first, with the on-device model as a fallback. Turn off cloud processing in Customize to use the local model. Cloud requests need internet access and are subject to Apple's usage limits.

YOUR PRIVATE NOTEBOOK
No app account is needed. Your entries stay on your device and sync through your private iCloud account when iCloud is available and enabled. The developer has no server that receives your journal. On Apple Watch, swipe through moods and add a quick note; Watch entries are stored locally and do not currently sync with the main app.

ABOUT PLUS AND THEME PURCHASES
Weekly, monthly, and yearly auto-renewing Plus subscriptions include the same premium features. Individual theme purchases are permanent and do not include AI. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Ending Plus keeps your entries and themes purchased separately.

MoodMargins is for personal reflection, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
